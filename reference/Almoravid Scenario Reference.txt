SCENARIO F — Reconquista and Riposte, 1085–1086
Length: Up to 14 turns (boxes 1–14), with Winter Sequence in boxes 7–8 and possible Curias-driven skip from Autumn (box 5 or 6) to Winter (box 7). Without Curias firing: full 14 turns. With Curias firing in box 5: 12 turns. With Curias firing in box 6: 13 turns.
Starting VP: Christian 7, Muslim 10
 
Map:
- Parias in Toledo, Badajoz, Granada, Valencia, Zaragoza, and Lérida Taifas.
- Alfonso, Pedro Ansúrez, and García Ordóñez at Sahagún.
- Álvar Fáñez, one yellow Siege, yellow Ravaged, and three Jihad markers at Toledo City.
- Al-Mutamid at Sevilla City.
- Yellow Ravaged at Talavera.
- Two Jihad markers each at Calatrava and Uclés.
- One Jihad marker at Trujillo.
 
Calendar:
- Box 1 has Levy marker and Sancho, Abu Bakr, al-Mustain, al-Mundir, al-Mutawakkil, Abd Allah, and Rodrigo al-Sayyid (green) cylinders.
- Box 4 has Alfonso, Álvar Fáñez, Pedro Ansúrez, and García Ordóñez Service markers.
- Box 6 has al-Mutamid Service marker.
- Box 7 has yellow Victory marker.
- Box 9 has Yusuf and Sir cylinders.
- Box 10 has green Victory marker.
- Box 15 has Scenario End marker.
 
Taifas Box: 6 green Conquered 1VP markers.
 
Mats — Christian:
- Alfonso
- Pedro Ansúrez
- García Ordóñez
- Álvar Fáñez
 
Mats — Muslim:
- Al-Mutamid
 
Set Aside: (none listed)
 
Special Rules:
- Events: Muslims start holding TAIFA MARRIAGE Event card.
- Capabilities: CATHEDRALS may not place Seats until Muster of Yusuf or Sir.
- Ruined Land: Parias Coin (1.4.3) awards Coin equal to Service less the number of Ravaged markers (of either side) in the Taifa.
- 1085–1086 Sequence: Apply 6.2 Curias and 6.3 Winter rules to the scenario's first Autumn and Winter, respectively. (See "Curias / Winter Procedure" below.)
- 6.3.5 Arts of War: In the first Spring Levy (box 9), draw Capabilities instead of Events (3.1).
- Standard Call to Arms (3.5) applies.
 
Curias / Winter Procedure (Scenario F only):
 
6.2 Curias — Check at start of first Autumn Levy (box 5); if not triggered, check again at start of second Autumn Levy (box 6).
- Curias condition: Locales (not the Taifas Box) have more total yellow (Christian) Conquered + Ravaged markers than green (Muslim) Conquered + Ravaged + Jihad markers (not VP).
- If condition met, Christians MUST: place a Curias marker in the current Levy's box (and a second in box 6 if firing in box 5); remove one 1VP Conquered from the Taifas Box per Curias marker placed (adjust Victory); advance Levy marker to box 7 (skip 1 or 2 boxes); shift Service markers of Beyond-Service Lords (in box 6 or lower) to box 7; if Pedro Ansúrez and/or García Ordóñez are on map, Disband them to the Calendar (3.3.2); proceed with Winter sequence.
 
6.3 Winter — Boxes 7 and 8 use Winter Disband / Winter Siege / Spring Muster / Plowing instead of normal Levy/Campaign.
- 6.3.1 Winter Disband (box 7): Pay Lords; remove any Beyond Service (exception: Rodrigo). All remaining Mustered Lords except those at Sieges Disband as if at Service limit, modified: clear each mat and place each cylinder on his mat (not Calendar); Disbanding Taifa Lords put all Coin from their mats into the Taifas Box (do not adjust Taifa status or award Parias Coin); if Disbanding either Rodrigo, place his cylinder at Calendar box 9 (not on mat) even if Beyond Service. Then discard all board-edge Capabilities.
- 6.3.2 Winter Siege (boxes 7 and 8): Each Besieging Lord may take one Supply or Ravage action; each Lord at a Siege Locale Feeds; then Christian, then Muslim, may Pay Lords at Siege and must Disband Lords at Siege Locales at or beyond Service limit.
- 6.3.3 Spring Muster (end of box 8): Christian Lords who Disbanded to mats automatically Muster — place cylinders at free Seats, Service markers ahead the normal number of boxes, prepare mats anew. If Alfonso Musters, he does so at León if possible. Lords with no free Seat do not Muster; place cylinder ahead on Calendar as if Disbanded this turn. Then Muslim Lords who Disbanded to mats and have a free Seat automatically Muster the same way; Taifa Lords with no free Seat go on the Calendar per 3.3.2 and adjust Taifa status (including any award of Parias Coin).
- 6.3.4 Plowing (end of box 8): Each Lord at a Siege reduces Carts and Mules each to ½ (round up).
