================================================================
ALMORAVID — STRATEGIC NOTES
Status: PLAYER HEURISTICS, not rules
Precedence: errata > rules > references > THIS FILE
================================================================

These are observations from played scenarios and from published
testers' replays and player commentary, recorded for faster
decision-making in future play. They are NOT rules, NOT errata,
and NOT authoritative. Where a heuristic appears to conflict
with rules or scenario-specific conditions, the rules and
scenario text govern. Treat each note as "consider this" rather
than "do this."

The notes assume normal play. Bidding, Hidden Mats, Vassal
Service advanced rule, and unusual Capability mixes can
invalidate any of them.

Sources cited in [brackets]:
  [BB]   = Almoravid Background Book strategy section
  [MC1]  = Mío Cid Testers' Replay Part I (Dreher/Campbell, InsideGMT)
  [MC2]  = Mío Cid Testers' Replay Part II
  [MC3]  = Mío Cid Testers' Replay Part III
  [SR]   = Reconquista and Riposte Full Scenario Replay
            (Correia/Campbell/Ruhnke, InsideGMT)
  [REV]  = Player reviews (TWBG, Ellis-Gorman, etc.)
  [PLAY] = Direct play observations from this project

SECTION 1 — TIMING & CALENDAR
================================================================

1.1  GROW REDUCES YELLOW AND GREEN RAVAGE MARKERS BY HALF AT
     END OF SPRING'S SECOND 40 DAYS. [PLAY]
     Consider: Ravage commands placed in Spring 1 or Spring 2
     lose roughly half their VP to Grow. Ravages placed in
     Summer or Autumn keep their full value (Grow only fires
     at end of Spring). In short scenarios that end before the
     next Spring, Autumn Ravages are worth their face value.
     Caveat: Enforcing Parias Service shifts happen at
     placement and aren't reversed by Grow.

1.2  HARVEST CUTS CARTS AND MULES IN HALF AT END OF SUMMER'S
     SECOND 40 DAYS. [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord with 1 Cart + 1 Mule entering Harvest
     keeps both (ceil(1/2)=1 each). Stockpiling Transport
     before Harvest is wasted; Levying replacements after
     Harvest costs Lordship actions but preserves them.

1.3  WASTAGE TRIGGERS ON "MORE THAN ONE OF ANY ASSET TYPE OR
     CAPABILITY CARD" BUT REMOVES ONLY ONE ITEM PER LORD. [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord stockpiling 6 Provender loses 1 to
     Wastage but the rest survive. The penalty is small per
     turn and easily managed by spreading Assets across Lords.

1.4  DISBAND DURING CAMPAIGN PLACES CYLINDER AT NEXT_BOX +
     SERVICE, NOT CURRENT_BOX + SERVICE (errata p.12). [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord pushed Beyond Service mid-Campaign is
     PERMANENTLY REMOVED. A Lord exactly at Service Limit
     mid-Campaign Disbands forward. The distinction is
     critical — Beyond Service means cylinder, mat, Seat
     markers all out of the game.

1.5  "EVERY SHIFT IN SERVICE LIMIT BECOMES MORE AND MORE
     IMPORTANT" LATE IN A SCENARIO. [SR]
     Consider: A Service shift one box left during Spring is
     usually recoverable; the same shift in late Autumn often
     means permanent loss. Plan Pay Coin reserves around the
     final two turns rather than spreading them evenly.

1.6  SPRING IS FOR TRANSPORT AND POSITIONING; SUMMER AND
     AUTUMN ARE FOR VASSALS AND OBJECTIVES. [MC3, John Campbell]
     Consider: "In spring you should go heavy on Transport and
     move Lords near to the areas of operations you want them
     in. In Spring and Summer, go for Vassals and push on your
     goals." Capabilities and Vassals lose value if there's no
     time left to use them.

SECTION 2 — LOGISTICS & FEED
================================================================

2.1  FEED CONSUMPTION SCALES IN STEPS OF 6. [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord with 6 mouths Feeds for 1 Provender; 7
     mouths Feeds for 2. Mustering one extra Vassal can double
     a Lord's Feed cost if it crosses the 6/7 threshold.
     Counting mouths before Mustering Vassals avoids surprises.

2.2  MULES COUNT AS MOUTHS; CARTS DO NOT. [PLAY]
     Consider: A "spare" Mule levied for Transport is also a
     Feed obligation. Carts have no Feed cost but are Laden on
     Passes if carrying Provender. The choice between Cart and
     Mule is route-dependent.

2.3  TORMES, SOMOSIERRA, AND OTHER PASS-ONLY CHOKEPOINTS
     PUNISH CARTS. [PLAY]
     Consider: Any route through a Pass-only Locale doubles
     March cost for a Cart with Provender. Mules are not
     Laden by Cart-on-Pass rules but ARE Laden if carrying 2+
     Provender. Discarding Cart contents before a Pass (rule
     1.7.2 Greed) is sometimes the cheapest move.

2.4  GARDENS AT FRIENDLY CITIES AND FORTRESSES AUTO-FORAGE.
     [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord at his Seat or any Friendly City/
     Fortress with no commands can stockpile 1 Provender per
     action with no roll. Two Lords stacked at a Garden
     Stronghold both auto-Forage independently.

2.5  PAY CANNOT BRIDGE LOCALES (rule 3.2.1). [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord stranded with 0 Coin and a Service
     marker about to expire cannot be saved by Coin on a
     distant ally's mat. Christians can plan stacking; Muslims
     can use the Taifas Box to Pay any Unbesieged Muslim Lord.

2.6  TAIFAS BOX COIN CANNOT PAY A BESIEGED LORD. [MC3, errata]
     Consider: Once a Muslim Lord is Besieged inside, Taifas
     Box Coin doesn't reach him. The change brings Almoravid
     in line with Nevsky's restriction. Fund Lords from the
     box BEFORE the Christian noose tightens.

2.7  "MAXING OUT" TROOPS CREATES MOBILITY DEBT. [BB]
     Consider: A fully Mustered Lord with all Vassals can need
     three Provender per Feed, requiring proximity to a Seat
     with adequate Transport. The choice is between a tough
     fixed garrison and a mobile small army. Don't max out
     Lords you intend to send raiding.

2.8  YUSUF STARTS WITH ONLY ONE MULE — HE CANNOT MARCH ON
     ARRIVAL. [MC2]
     Consider: To get Yusuf's massive army moving in his first
     turn, Pay him with Coin from the Taifas Box and have a
     nearby Taifa Lord Share Transport. Planning for Yusuf to
     act independently the turn he arrives is unrealistic.

SECTION 3 — CARDS & CAPABILITIES
================================================================

3.1  TAX CONSUMES THE ENTIRE CARD REGARDLESS OF COMMAND
     RATING. [PLAY]
     Consider: Capabilities that boost Command (Hasham,
     Mesnada, +1 to Lord) provide no benefit on Tax cards.
     Tax produces 1 Coin per card invested.

3.2  THE ARTS OF WAR DECK IS A "FACE-UP MENU OF SPECIAL
     POWERS" FOR CAPABILITY LEVY. [REV, There Will Be Games]
     Consider: First Levy of a scenario draws Capabilities at
     random (3.1.2). Subsequent Levies draw Events (3.1.3). A
     Lordship action during Muster (3.4.4) lets a Lord SELECT
     a Capability from the deck. Do not treat the deck as a
     surprise mechanism — players know its contents and
     should plan around them.

3.3  TWO "THIS LORD" CAPABILITIES IS THE PER-LORD MAXIMUM
     (rule 3.4.4). [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord at the cap can't Levy more "This Lord"
     cards. Wastage at End Campaign discards one item from any
     Lord with multiple of any type — that includes "more than
     one This Lord card" by literal reading. In practice,
     plan around Wastage by accepting the loss or by spreading
     "This Lord" cards across multiple Lords.

3.4  BISHOPRICS IS A +6-UNITS / +6-MOUTHS TRADE OVER THREE
     LORDS. [PLAY]
     Consider: Each Bishop Vassal brings 2 units (1 Knight +
     1 M-at-A or Militia). Three Bishops = 6 units total. The
     same three Lords gain 6 mouths in their Feed cycle. In
     long scenarios the combat power justifies the logistics
     cost; in 3-turn scenarios the mouths often outweigh the
     unit value.

3.5  UPHOLD THE DYNASTIES DELIVERS 1.5 VP PER LEVY WITH NO
     COMMITMENT (3.5.2 option [c]). [PLAY, MC2]
     Consider: While both Yusuf and Sir are Ready, the Muslim
     side gets +1 Conquered + 1 Jihad per Levy by declining
     them. Three Levies of Uphold = 4.5 VP, often the spine of
     Muslim victory in short scenarios. The cost is delaying
     Almoravid arrival — usually fine if Christians aren't
     pressing decisive Sieges. Phil Dreher (tester) noted he
     "could have comfortably just deferred them for the
     remainder of the game" in an aggressive Christian
     pressure scenario.

3.6  CALLING ONE ALMORAVID LOCKS IN UPHOLD-FOREGO. [MC2]
     Consider: "Once one of the Almoravids has mustered you
     are no longer eligible to earn points for deferring
     them." Call them in only when you have a concrete plan
     for their immediate use; otherwise the lost VP from
     deferral compounds.

3.7  AL-QADIR (Christian Event C20) REMOVES 2 JIHAD FROM A
     RECONQUISTA OR PARIAS TAIFA WITHOUT MUSLIM LORDS. [PLAY]
     Consider: Saves 1 Muslim VP at no cost. Toledo Taifa
     (Reconquista, no Muslim Lords) is the natural target;
     Calatrava with stacked Jihad is the highest-value
     individual Stronghold. NOTE [MC2]: Removing all Jihad
     from Toledo CITY itself can be a mistake — a Neutral
     Toledo cannot be Conquered, blocking Reconquista status
     gains. Leave at least one Jihad on Toledo City if you
     intend to retake it.

3.8  CATHEDRALS COMES WITH A JIHAD RIDER. [PLAY]
     Consider: Placing a Cathedral Seat marker yields +1
     Christian VP but lets Muslims add 1 Jihad of their
     choosing (worth 0.5 VP). Net +0.5 VP for Christian. The
     Seat marker is also a Friendly Locale anchor, which can
     enable Muster from Conquered Cities.

3.9  M14 DEVALUATION (Event side) HALVES CHRISTIAN COIN
     PER LOCALE, ROUND UP. [PLAY]
     Consider: Stacking Christian Lords at one Locale
     concentrates the loss (2 Coin + 1 Coin at same Locale →
     reduce to 2 = lose 1 Coin); spreading spreads it.
     Spending Coin on Pay before Devaluation can land is the
     cleanest hedge.

3.10 LEÓN Y CASTILLA IS A RECURRING SERVICE-DRAIN ON
     CHRISTIAN COUNTS. [MC1]
     Consider: This Muslim Event shifts the Service of one of
     four named Christian Lords (Pedro, García, Álvar,
     Rodrigo Campeador) and bans Muster of/by them this Levy.
     Phil Dreher noted "this card always comes up." Plan
     Christian Pay reserves to absorb at least one such hit
     per scenario; expect two in Scenario F.

3.11 BATTERING RAM AND BALLESTEROS ARE SCENARIO-DEFINING
     CHRISTIAN CAPABILITIES. [BB, MC1]
     Consider: "Expect a full seven-to-eight cards with
     Battering Rams to take down a City" without them, the
     Siege math becomes prohibitive. If Cities are on the
     target list, prioritize Battering Ram via early
     Capability Levy.

3.12 CABALGADAS / AL-GARRADA RAID-AT-RANGE IS CARDED. [MC2]
     Consider: Costs 1 Provender and the entire Command card,
     ravaging a Locale up to 2 Ways distant. Useful for El Cid
     and other high-Command Lords, but expensive enough that
     it's better as a "finish" play late in a turn rather than
     a routine action.

SECTION 4 — MOVEMENT & POSITIONING
================================================================

4.1  A BYPASSED STRONGHOLD IS NOT A BESIEGED STRONGHOLD. [PLAY]
     Consider: A Lord who Withdraws into a Stronghold while
     Enemy Bypasses outside is NOT Besieged. He retains full
     Command options — March out, Sortie, Forage, Tax — that
     a Besieged Lord lacks. Sally specifically requires
     Besieged status (Siege markers placed).

4.2  ENCAMP COSTS A FULL CARD AND ENDS ITS ACTIONS. [PLAY]
     Consider: To Storm a Bypassed Stronghold, the Christian
     must first Encamp (1 March action, replaces all Bypass
     with 1 Siege marker, ends card) and then Storm on a
     LATER card. Two cards minimum for Bypass-to-Storm.

4.3  STORM WITH 0 SIEGE MARKERS ENDS AFTER 0 ROUNDS. [PLAY]
     Consider: Rule 4.5.2 — Storm ends when Rounds completed
     equal Siege markers. Storm without prior Siege
     accomplishes nothing. Encamp first or Siege first.

4.4  A LORD AT AN ENEMY LOCALE CANNOT MUSTER. [PLAY]
     Consider: Bypass status doesn't make a Locale Friendly.
     Muster (3.4) requires Friendly Locale. A raiding Lord
     deep in Enemy Taifa cannot Levy Vassals or Capabilities
     until he returns to Friendly Territory.

4.5  RAVAGE ENFORCING PARIAS SHIFTS ON ODD-NUMBERED RAVAGE
     PER TAIFA, NOT PER LOCALE. [PLAY]
     Consider: The 1st, 3rd, 5th… Christian Ravage marker
     placed in a single Taifa each shifts that Taifa Lord's
     Service 1 box left. Rapidly raiding the same Taifa
     stacks pressure on its Lord. Spreading Ravages across
     multiple Taifas dilutes the effect but covers more
     ground.

4.6  WHEN A BESIEGER IS REMOVED, SIEGE MARKERS COME OFF
     (rule 4.3.5). [PLAY]
     Consider: A single Besieger who hits Beyond Service mid-
     Siege loses both himself and all Siege markers placed.
     Stack at least one supporting Lord before risking long
     Sieges, even if Capacity doesn't strictly require it.

4.7  NEVER LEAVE A SIEGE LORD ALONE — THE COUNTER-ATTACK IS
     THE STANDARD MUSLIM PLAY. [MC3, John Campbell]
     Consider: Campbell explicitly cites his loss of Toledo
     Siegeworks to al-Mutamid's counter-attack: "Calamity
     ensues when al-Mutamid strikes and drives Pedro from the
     Siege." Always station two Lords at any non-trivial
     Siege, ideally with at least one able to Battle if
     Sallied or relieved.

4.8  RAVAGE-THEN-SIEGE BENEFITS THE BESIEGER, BUT FLIPS ON
     CONQUEST. [BB]
     Consider: Ravaging a Locale you'll Besiege gives +1 each
     to Surrender odds (your Ravage marker counts) and adds
     Provender/Loot from Rustling. But once you Conquer, the
     Ravage flips to ½ VP for the Enemy. Net VP swing is
     usually still positive for the Besieger; the calculus
     changes if Conquest is uncertain.

4.9  RAVAGE-AND-PIN IS THE PROVEN MUSLIM CONTAINMENT.
     [SR, BB]
     Consider: When the Christian Lord can't be defeated in
     the field, Bypass him and Ravage his retreat path. This
     was used to isolate García Ordóñez in Lérida Taifa
     during Scenario F testing — Jaca-Huesca corridor blocked
     by Jihad and Sieges, García's path home blocked.

4.10 GUADALQUIVIR IS TAIFA-LORDS-ONLY; AFRICAN FLEET IS
     UNIVERSAL. [MC2]
     Consider: The Guadalquivir Capability allows long-range
     Port-to-Port movement but only for Taifa Lords. The same
     card's Event side (African Fleet) allows the same for any
     Lord including Yusuf and Sir. Holding the Event for
     Yusuf's arrival is often more valuable than playing the
     Capability early.

SECTION 5 — VICTORY MATH
================================================================

5.1  STRONGHOLD CONQUEST DICE SCALE WITH VP VALUE. [PLAY]
     Castle/Town: 1 die ≤ Siege+Ravaged.
     Fortress:    2 dice, all must pass.
     City:        3 dice, all must pass.
     Consider: Cities and Fortresses rarely Surrender —
     Storming is usually the realistic path. Castles/Towns
     can fall to Surrender with 2-3 Siege markers and patience.

5.2  CONQUERING A STRONGHOLD MOVES MARKERS BUT ALSO FLIPS
     RAVAGE. [PLAY]
     Consider: A Christian Conquering a previously Muslim-
     Ravaged Locale flips that ½ VP from green to yellow —
     net 1 VP swing per flipped Ravage in addition to the
     Conquered marker.

5.3  RECONQUISTA TAIFA = 3 VP. PARIAS TAIFA = 1 VP. [PLAY]
     Consider: A Taifa shift from Independent to Parias is +1
     Christian. From Parias to Reconquista is +2 more (3-1).
     Reconquista requires Conquering all printed Taifa Lord
     Seats (and the Taifa's Cities, where applicable). One
     unconquered Seat keeps the Taifa at most at Parias.

5.4  JIHAD MARKERS ARE INDIVIDUALLY 0.5 VP BUT COMPOUND. [PLAY]
     Consider: A Stronghold with 4 Jihad markers is worth 2 VP
     to the Muslim side AND makes the Locale Muslim-Friendly,
     which can make it eligible for further Muslim activity.
     Christians clearing a single Locale's stacked Jihad
     (Conquest, Al-Qadir) reclaims the VP at once.

5.5  RETAKING TOLEDO IS A 6-VP SWING. [SR]
     Consider: Yusuf retaking Toledo City converted: -3 yellow
     Conquered (replaced with Jihad), +0.5 each Jihad placed,
     -2 Reconquista (Toledo back to Parias = -3 reverting to
     +1), and flipped Ravage credit = ~6 VP total swing. This
     is the single most decisive moment available to the
     Muslim side in Scenario F. Any Christian plan for Toledo
     must consider how to defend it after taking it.

5.6  WITHOUT TOLEDO OR EQUIVALENT, "ALL IS FOR NAUGHT."
     [MC3, John Campbell]
     Consider: Final board states with strong armies, ravaged
     enemy lands, and expanded borders can still lose if no
     City-tier prize was secured. Ravage VP and Conquered
     markers compound to a point — beyond it, only Reconquista
     or Cathedrals deliver winning margins.

SECTION 6 — SCENARIO-SPECIFIC OBSERVATIONS
================================================================

6.1  SCENARIO C (PARIAS WARS) FAVORS THE MUSLIM DEFENSIVE
     POSTURE BY ABOUT 0.5–1 VP. [PLAY]
     Consider: Three Levies of Uphold the Dynasties (4.5 VP)
     plus stable Taifa boundaries usually outpace Christian
     raiding, especially with Spring Grow halving early
     Ravages. Christian needs either an early decisive Storm
     (rare with starting forces) or Autumn-weighted raiding
     (impossible — scenario ends at start of Summer). Bidding
     for sides is appropriate if balance matters.

6.2  IN SHORT SCENARIOS, MUSLIM LORDSHIP 1 IS BINDING. [PLAY]
     Consider: With one Levy action per Lord and no Capability
     to spend on, Muslims default to "Muster one Vassal."
     This is fine — Muslim Lords are typically defending
     Seats, where extra Vassals help Storm defense without
     extending campaigns.

6.3  ALFONSO'S STARTING FORCES (1 Knight + 1 M-at-A + 1 Serf)
     ARE THIN. [PLAY]
     Consider: Don't expect Alfonso to anchor Battles alone
     until he Musters Froila Bermudez (3 units) and ideally
     Vela Ovequez (2 units). Until then he's a Marshal
     coordinating a small household.

6.4  SANCHO'S RAID-AND-RETURN PATTERN IS RELIABLE. [PLAY]
     Consider: Jaca → Pamplona → Calahorra → Tudela is a 3-
     action raid axis. Sancho's modest Force size is a
     liability in Battle but ideal for Bypass-Ravage cycles
     in Independent Zaragoza. Ravage VP from Sancho often
     decides ½-VP scenarios.

6.5  SCENARIO F: TOLEDO CAN FALL ON TURN 1 WITH BATTERING
     RAM AND LUCK. [SR]
     Consider: The full scenario opens with Alfonso and three
     lords already Besieging Toledo. A Battering Ram
     Capability and a fortunate Surrender roll can take the
     City in two Siege actions. Christian opening play should
     prioritize this — it converts the turn-1 1-VP Parias
     marker to a 3-VP Reconquista plus 3 VP in Conquered
     markers, swinging 5+ VP immediately.

6.6  SCENARIO F MUSLIM EARLY-GAME IS DEFENSIVE BANKING. [BB]
     Consider: "Early Campaigns are going to be tough on the
     Muslims as Alfonso VI and his loyal Lords will run around
     Ravaging and Sieging." The right play is often to let an
     overrun Taifa Lord Disband and pay Parias rather than
     fight a losing field battle. Allowing one Taifa to fall
     while banking Coin in the Taifas Box for an explosive
     Almoravid arrival is a documented tester strategy.

6.7  TAIFA MARRIAGE EXTENDS A LORD'S SERVICE BY 1 BOX. [MC1]
     Consider: A Hold Event the Muslims can use to save a
     Taifa Lord on the verge of Disband. Holding Taifa
     Marriage through a quiet stretch lets it cover the
     critical late-Autumn Service crunch.

SECTION 7 — LORD-SPECIFIC NOTES
================================================================

7.1  AL-MUTAMID IS THE MUSLIM SPEARPOINT. [Geography article,
     BB]
     Consider: "Without the Taifa of Sevilla and its
     militarily capable Lord as a spearpoint, the Muslim
     player will be hard-pressed to stem the Christian tide
     — even once the Almoravid army appears on the scene."
     Al-Mutamid's Service of 6 (vs 4 for other Taifa Lords)
     means he can stay in the field through long campaigns.
     Protect him; lose him and the Muslim defense collapses.

7.2  EL CID IS HIGH-RISK / HIGH-REWARD. [BB]
     Consider: "Employ him as soon as I can afford it
     operationally. El Cid is high-risk/high-reward in the
     sense that you may hire him and then have an Event pull
     him away. Personally, however, I think the rewards
     usually outweigh the risks." The Christian player can
     also Reconcile with him to deny him to the Muslim side
     even at VP cost — permanent Lord-denial is often worth
     2 VP.

7.3  EL CID CAN STORM WITH JUST HIS STARTING SIX UNITS. [BB]
     Consider: For tempo, El Cid can move and fight without
     any Vassal Muster. His Command of 4 makes him the
     long-range raid specialist via Cabalgadas. Saving him
     "lean" preserves Lordship for other Lords.

7.4  PEDRO ANSÚREZ IS ALFONSO'S LIFELONG FRIEND BUT
     STRUCTURALLY VULNERABLE. [SR]
     Consider: Pedro at Simancas is far from his expected
     theater of operations (Toledo, Zaragoza). He's the
     classic "second Christian Lord" stuck supplying or
     raiding the periphery. Keep him paired with another Lord
     whenever possible — solo Pedro has been documented
     repeatedly as the Muslim counter-raid target.

7.5  GARCÍA ORDÓÑEZ IS THE EAST-FRONT RAIDER. [SR]
     Consider: García's Seat at Nájera positions him for raids
     into Zaragoza and Lérida Taifas. His Command of 2 limits
     campaign depth, but as a Lieutenant under Alférez (C15)
     he can ride with another Lord for combined operations.

7.6  YUSUF NEEDS TRANSPORT BEFORE HE CAN CAMPAIGN. [MC2]
     Consider: Starting with 1 Mule and a 14-unit army, Yusuf
     can't Feed and March on his arrival turn. Plan for either
     a Pay-to-Move sequence or a Levy of Carts via Lordship
     before sending him north. The DAWUD IBN AISHA + AL-
     YAZIRAT AL-HADRA combination (3 Provender per Supply via
     double Seat) is the documented Yusuf supply strategy.

SECTION 8 — PROCEDURAL TRAPS & PRE-COMMIT CHECKS
================================================================

8.1  Before Mustering a Vassal, count post-Muster mouths and
     Provender on hand. Crossing a Feed threshold mid-Campaign
     starves Lords.

8.2  Before March, confirm Laden status by counting Provender,
     Loot, and Cart/Mule load relative to Way type.

8.3  Before Approach, confirm whether the Defender will
     Withdraw or Stand. Withdrawal forces Bypass/Siege choice
     and ends the March action.

8.4  Before Encamp, confirm support is en route. A solo
     Encamp without follow-up Siege/Storm cards wastes the
     conversion.

8.5  Before End Campaign, count surviving Ravage markers and
     project Grow/Harvest losses. Adjust Plan stacks to time
     Ravages favorably.

8.6  Before Pay during Levy, identify which Lords need Coin
     and whether Coin-bearing Lords are at the same Locale.
     Re-stacking happens during Campaign March, not Levy.

8.7  Before Call to Arms, check whether Reconcile/Crusade/
     Reconcile-with-al-Sayyid options have triggers met. The
     "decline" reward (Uphold the Dynasties) is the Muslim
     default; the Christian "decline" yields no VP.

8.8  Before Storming, confirm Siege markers placed. Storm
     Round count = Siege marker count. Zero markers, zero
     rounds, no effect.

8.9  Before removing Jihad from a Toledo City target, confirm
     Conquest plans. A Neutral Toledo cannot be Conquered.
     [MC2]

8.10 Before Inviting an Almoravid, plan his first turn's
     supply. Yusuf or Sir on the map without Coin or Transport
     is a wasted Call to Arms. [MC2]

SECTION 9 — META-OBSERVATIONS ON PLAY STYLE
================================================================

9.1  "TRYING TO PUT TOO MANY BALLS IN THE AIR IS A SUREFIRE
     WAY TO DROP ALL OF THEM." [MC3, John Campbell]
     Consider: Almoravid rewards focused offensives over broad
     pressure. Two armies committed to one objective beat four
     armies splitting four ways.

9.2  "EVERY LOSS IS DEFINITIVE, AND EVERY VICTORY COUNTS
     DOUBLE." [SR]
     Consider: Late-scenario play should weight conservatism.
     Refusing Battle to preserve Lords in the final two turns
     is often correct even when offensively favored.

9.3  FIELD BATTLES ARE RARE AND DECISIVE. [REV, MC3]
     Consider: "Many 40-day periods will not see any battles
     at all." A Battle commits both sides' Forces; Avoiding
     Battle costs Assets but preserves the army. Only Battle
     when you have decisive advantage in numbers, Capabilities,
     or held Events.

9.4  CASTLES ARE TIME SINKS. [REV]
     Consider: "Castles can take a truly staggering amount of
     time, luck and/or bodies to overcome." Avoid Castle
     Sieges unless they are scenario-critical. Towns and
     Fortresses are usually better targets per action invested.

9.5  IF YOU CAN'T DEFEND A TAIFA, LET IT GO. [BB]
     Consider: "It can be a good choice if you cannot defend
     a Taifa to let that Taifa Lord Disband, go Parias and pay
     tribute, lest all the Locales there get Ravaged."
     Conceding a Taifa swap (Independent → Parias = +1 VP
     Christian, but stops the bleeding) sometimes preserves
     more than fighting for it.

9.6  CONSIDER HIDDEN MATS AND VASSAL SERVICE ADVANCED RULES.
     [BB]
     Consider: "Playing with advanced Service markers and
     hidden mats really ups the game, and, honestly, I don't
     know if I myself would play it any other way." These
     change strategy substantially — Service shifts hit
     Vassals as well, and opponent Capabilities become hidden
     until used. Advanced play is more historically textured.
