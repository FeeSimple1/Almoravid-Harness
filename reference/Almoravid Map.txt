============================================================
 PART 4 — TAIFA STATUS BOXES
============================================================
 
Each Taifa has a status-tracking box (or boxes) printed
within its territory. The default state shows "Independent."
When status changes per rule 1.4.1, the box is covered with
a Parias marker or a Reconquista marker.
 
  Badajoz   — 1 box, "Independent"
  Toledo    — 1 box, "Parias or Reconquista"
              (Toledo has no Taifa Lord and can NEVER be
               Independent — rule 1.4.1 note. Starts as
               Reconquista in Scenarios B, C, D, E and as
               Parias in Scenarios A and F.)
  Zaragoza  — 1 box, "Independent"
  Lérida    — 1 box, "Independent"
  Valencia  — 1 box, "Independent"
  Sevilla   — 3 boxes, all "Independent"
              (Sevilla is worth 3× VP of other Taifas;
               three markers placed when status changes)
  Granada   — 1 box, "Independent"
 
VP per Taifa status:
  Independent  → 0 VP
  Parias       → 1 VP Christian (3 VP if Sevilla)
  Reconquista  → 3 VP Christian (9 VP if Sevilla)

 PART 6 — KEY OBSERVATIONS
============================================================
 
CHOKEPOINTS:
 
- SOMOSIERRA: Connects only via 4 Passes (Madrid,
  Peñafiel, Simancas, Guadarrama). NO ROADS at all.
  Primary central-meseta crossing for north-south
  movement, but every transit Ladens Carts.
 
- TORMES: Connects only via 2 Passes (Coria, Transduero).
  No Roads. The León→Extremadura corridor.
 
- GORMAZ: Only 2 connections — Peñafiel (Road) and
  Medinaceli (Pass to Zaragoza). Eastern León Fortress
  anchoring the Duero frontier.
 
- SOUTHERN MOUNTAINS: 10 Passes link Granada Taifa to
  Sevilla Taifa — heavily connected, but every link
  is a Pass.
 
ISOLATED / DISTINCTIVE:
 
- ALMERÍA: Three land Ways, all Passes (Málaga, Granada,
  Lorca). Plus Guadalquivir as a Port.
 
- CARTAGENA: Two land Ways, both Roads (Aledo,
  Alicante). Plus Guadalquivir as a Port.
 
- MORÓN — MÁLAGA: The only Road crossing from Sevilla
  Taifa into Granada Taifa.
 
HUBS:
 
- TOLEDO (city): 5 spokes — Talavera, Calatrava, Madrid,
  Uclés, Zancara (all Roads). Most connected stronghold
  in central Iberia.
 
- ZARAGOZA (city): 4 spokes — Tudela, Huesca, Calatayud,
  Ibérico (all Roads).
 
- SEVILLA (city): 5 spokes — Córdoba, Jerez, Huelva,
  Morón, Ardila (all Roads). Plus Atlantic port.
 
- BURGOS: Christian Seat-of-seats — Alfonso AND Álvar
  Fáñez both have printed pennants here. The most
  important Christian Stronghold for Muster purposes
  (two Lords can Muster directly from it).
 
- ALGECIRAS: Muslim entry-point Stronghold for Yusuf
  and Sir (the Almoravid Marshal and his cousin).
  When Yusuf is Called to Arms ("Invite the Almoravids"),
  he Musters at Algeciras. If Algeciras is unfriendly or
  Besieged, falls back to nearest Friendly siege-free
  Port. The AL-YAZIRAT AL-HADRA capability gives Yusuf
  a doubled Seat marker here.
 
- ALEDO: A small Castle with 4 connections (Lorca,
  Murcia, Cartagena via Road; Alcaraz via Pass). Anchor
  for the southeast.
 
CORIA TRIANGLE: Coria has 3 Roads (Alcántara, Mérida,
Talavera) plus the Tormes Pass. León's forward base for
projection into Extremadura.
