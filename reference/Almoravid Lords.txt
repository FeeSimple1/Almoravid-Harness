================================================================
ALMORAVID — LORD REFERENCE  (v4, with Scenario F notes and
                              Call to Arms reference)
================================================================
 
Ratings key:
  Fealty   — Die-roll range to Muster (— = no Fealty, Call to Arms only)
  Service  — 40-Days boxes ahead on Calendar at Muster/Disband
  Lordship — Levy actions per Muster segment
  Command  — Actions per Command card during Campaign
 
Seats key:
  "Seats" lists the printed pennants on the map for that Lord.
  These count for Muster (3.4.1), Supply (4.6.1), Tax (4.7.3),
  Call Upon an Emir (3.5.2), and — for the six Muslim Taifa
  Lords only — Reconquista targets (1.4.1).
  Yusuf, Sir, and Rodrigo are NOT Taifa Lords; their Seats
  do not count toward Reconquista targets even though printed.
 
Scenario F additions (Reconquista and Riposte, full-length):
  Several entries below carry "SCENARIO F:" notes covering
  Curias (6.2), Winter Disband (6.3.1), and Spring Muster
  (6.3.3). These rules apply only in Scenario F, and only
  to specific Lords. See the Scenario Adjustments document
  for setup-level items (starting Coin, Capability cards
  pre-assigned to mats, Ruined Land Parias Coin formula).

================================================================
SPECIAL VASSALS  (Christian-side only)
================================================================
 
Special Vassals (1.5.4, 3.4.2) enter play via Arts of War cards
rather than starting on a Lord's mat. They have no Service
Rating — ignore the printed numerals on the markers. Combat
loss of their units leaves the Vassal marker in place; it does
not Disband the Vassal.
 
Advanced Rule exemption (3.4.2): Bishops and Crusaders are
NEVER tracked on the Calendar, even when the Vassal Service
advanced rule is in use. Their markers stay on the Lord's mat
and are removed only when the Lord Disbands or per card text
(e.g., Bishoprics discarded → all Bishops removed from play).
 
Fonsadera (C23): exchanges Ready non-Bishop Vassals only.
Bishops cannot be exchanged for Coin or Transport.

================================================================
CALL TO ARMS REFERENCE  (Rule 3.5)
================================================================
 
Call to Arms is a Levy segment that lets each side bring in
Lords who have no Fealty rating — Yusuf, Sir, Eudes, and the
two Rodrigos. These Lords cannot be Mustered through ordinary
Fealty rolls (3.4.1); Call to Arms is the ONLY way they enter
play. The Christian options (3.5.1) resolve first, then the
Muslim options (3.5.2). Each side may take one option or pass.
 
PLAY NOTE (per 6.1): Scenarios A and B skip Call to Arms
entirely. The relevant Lords are absent in those scenarios.
Skip section 3.5 if playing A or B.
 
General requirement (3.5 footnote): To select any Call to Arms
option, a side must possess any Coin, free Seat, or Jihad
marker that the chosen option calls for.
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
"READY" — WHEN A CALL-TO-ARMS LORD CAN BE BROUGHT IN
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
Most options require their target Lord to be "Ready" per 3.4.1
— meaning his cylinder is on the Calendar (at or to the left
of the current Levy box), coat-of-arms face up, awaiting
Muster. A cylinder that is in the box, set aside, or already
on a Mustered Lord's mat is not Ready.
 
How each Call-to-Arms Lord becomes Ready:
 
  YUSUF                  — Scenario setup only.
  SIR                    — Scenario setup only.
  RODRIGO AL-SAYYID      — Scenario setup only (green cylinder
   (Muslim, green)         on the Calendar).
  RODRIGO CAMPEADOR      — Scenario setup OR via the Christian
   (Christian, yellow)     "Reconcile with Rodrigo" option,
                           which places his yellow cylinder on
                           the Calendar 2 boxes ahead of the
                           current Levy.
  EUDES                  — Scenario setup OR triggered by the
                           Muslim "Invite the Almoravids"
                           option, which places Eudes' cylinder
                           on the Calendar 2 boxes ahead. Per
                           the rule's NOTE, those are the only
                           two ways Eudes can become Ready.
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
3.5.1  CHRISTIAN OPTIONS  ("Campeador or Crusade?")
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
Christians act first. Take one option or pass.
 
[1] RECONCILE WITH RODRIGO
    Trigger:  Rodrigo al-Sayyid (green) is on the map, OR
              Disband or combat has permanently removed any
              Christian Lord (3.3.1, 4.4.4, 4.5.2).
    Cost:     1 green 1VP Conquered marker added to the Taifas
              box, PLUS 1VP for each Calendar box that Rodrigo
              al-Sayyid's Service marker is ahead of the
              current Levy (adjust Victory, 5.1).
    Effect:   Disband Rodrigo al-Sayyid (if on map). Set aside
              his green cylinder and Seat marker. Place Rodrigo
              Campeador's yellow cylinder onto the Calendar
              2 boxes ahead of the current Levy.
    Example:  On Levy of turn 4, Rodrigo's green Service marker
              is in box 6 → Reconcile costs 3VP (1 base + 2 for
              the 2 boxes ahead).
 
[2] EMPLOY RODRIGO  (Christian-side)
    Trigger:  Rodrigo Campeador (yellow) is Ready.
    Cost:     2 Coin total, paid from any Unbesieged Christian
              Lord mats.
    Effect:   Place the yellow Rodrigo Seat marker (from
              wherever it currently is) at a Christian-Friendly
              Stronghold that is free of Siege. Automatically
              Muster Rodrigo Campeador there.
 
[3] CALL FOR CRUSADE
    Trigger:  Eudes is Ready AND Pamplona is Christian-Friendly
              AND Pamplona is free of Siege.
    Cost:     None for the Christian side. The Muslim player
              may then add 1 Jihad marker to the map if able
              (1.4.4, adjust Victory).
    Effect:   Automatically Muster Eudes at Pamplona.
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
3.5.2  MUSLIM OPTIONS  ("al-Sayyid or al-Murabitun?")
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
Muslims act second. Take one option or pass.
 
[1] EMPLOY RODRIGO  (Muslim-side)
    Trigger:  Rodrigo al-Sayyid (green) is Ready.
    Cost:     3 Coin total, paid from the Taifas box and/or any
              Unbesieged Muslim Lord mats.
    Effect:   Place the green Rodrigo Seat marker (from
              wherever it currently is) at a Muslim-Friendly
              Stronghold that is free of Siege. Automatically
              Muster Rodrigo al-Sayyid there.
 
[2] INVITE THE ALMORAVIDS
    Trigger:  Yusuf OR Sir is Ready.
    Cost:     None directly, but adds Eudes to the Christian
              roster as Ready (see Effect).
    Effect:   Place the single Seat marker for the chosen Lord
              (Yusuf or Sir) at Algeciras and automatically
              Muster him there. If Algeciras is not Muslim-
              Friendly or is Besieged, use the nearest Port
              that is Muslim-Friendly and free of Siege. THEN,
              if Eudes is not already on the Calendar or
              Mustered, place Eudes' cylinder on the Calendar
              2 boxes ahead of the current Levy.
    NOTE:     If AL-YAZIRAT AL-HADRA capability is in play
              (1.9.1), use the doubled Seat marker rather than
              the single one.
    Ports list (for the fallback Stronghold):
      Mediterranean: Algeciras, Málaga, Almería, Cartagena,
                     Alicante, Denia, Valencia, Tortosa.
      Atlantic:      Jerez, Huelva, Sevilla.
      River:         Zaragoza, Lérida.
 
[3] UPHOLD THE DYNASTIES
    Trigger:  BOTH Yusuf AND Sir are Ready (cylinders on the
              Calendar at or left of the current Levy box).
    Cost:     Shift both Yusuf and Sir cylinders into the
              40-Days box just to the right of the current
              Levy box (delays Almoravid intervention by at
              least one turn).
    Effect:   Add 1 green 1VP Conquered marker to the Taifas
              box AND 1 Jihad marker to the map (if able,
              1.4.4; adjust Victory).
    Read as:  The Taifas trade away one turn of Almoravid
              availability for VP and a Jihad bump.
 
[4] CALL UPON AN EMIR
    Trigger:  Yusuf is at a Taifa Lord's Seat (1.5.1) that is
              neither Enemy nor Besieged. The Taifa Lord at
              that Seat is the target.
    Cost:     None (beyond Yusuf already being on the Seat).
    Effect:   EITHER
              (a) Muster that Taifa Lord automatically (no
                  Fealty roll required) from any Calendar box,
                  OR
              (b) Shift that Taifa Lord's Service marker right
                  by 2 boxes.
    Read as:  Yusuf, present at a Taifa capital, exerts direct
              pressure — either calling that emir into the
              field or wearing him down (Service shift).
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
3.5.3  DISCARD EVENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
After both sides have resolved Call to Arms, both sides discard
any Events applying only to "This Levy" (3.1.3). This closes
out the Levy phase.

  Scenario A — No Call to Arms. Skip 3.5 entirely. Yusuf, Sir,
               Eudes, both Rodrigos, and several other Lords
               are explicitly Set Aside.
 
  Scenario B — No Call to Arms. Skip 3.5 entirely. Eudes,
               Yusuf, Sir, and each Rodrigo are explicitly
               Set Aside.
 
  Scenario C — Standard 3.5 applies. Yusuf, Sir, and Rodrigo
               al-Sayyid begin on Calendar box 9 (Ready from
               turn 1). Eudes and Rodrigo Campeador are NOT
               on the Calendar at start; each can only enter
               through the chains described above (Reconcile;
               Invite the Almoravids → later Call for Crusade).
 
  Scenario D — Standard 3.5 applies. Yusuf is already Mustered
               at Algeciras at scenario start. Sir and Rodrigo
               al-Sayyid begin on Calendar box 12 (the Levy
               box). Eudes begins on Calendar box 13. Rodrigo
               Campeador is not initially Ready (Reconcile
               only).
 
  Scenario E — Standard 3.5 applies. Same Ready set as C:
               Yusuf, Sir, and Rodrigo al-Sayyid on Calendar
               box 9. Eudes and Rodrigo Campeador are not
               initially Ready.
 
  Scenario F — Standard 3.5 applies. Rodrigo al-Sayyid begins
               on Calendar box 1; Yusuf and Sir on Calendar
               box 9. Eudes and Rodrigo Campeador are not
               initially Ready.

RELATED MECHANICS  (cross-references)
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
- "Call upon an Emir" (3.5.2 option [4]) is one of the few
  in-game ways to bypass the Fealty roll for a Taifa Lord.
  Cards or Events that mention "Call Upon an Emir" refer to
  this option.
 
- INDULGENCES (Event C11) and SONG OF ROLAND (Event C12) sit
  outside Call to Arms but interact with Eudes — each forces
  Eudes (if on map and Unbesieged) to immediately Muster all
  his Ready Vassals for no actions, and forces the Christians
  to Muster a Crusaders Special Vassal marker. See SPECIAL
  VASSALS section above.
 
- AL-YAZIRAT AL-HADRA (capability, 1.9.1) doubles Yusuf's and
  Sir's Algeciras Seat — when Inviting the Almoravids, use
  the double-Seat marker if this capability is in play.
 
- Eudes-via-Invite-the-Almoravids: this places Eudes on the
  Calendar but does NOT immediately Muster him. The Christians
  must still execute "Call for Crusade" on a later turn to
  bring Eudes into play (and only if Pamplona is then
  Christian-Friendly and free of Siege).
 
- Both Rodrigos share forces, ratings, Vassals, and Command
  cards (the same physical Lord, employed by different sides).
  Only one Rodrigo is on the map at a time. Reconcile is the
  only way to switch him from Muslim service to Christian
  service mid-game.
 
DESIGN NOTE (3.5): The VP added in Call to Arms represent the
prestige cost for King Alfonso to reverse his exile of Rodrigo,
or the dynastic security that the emirs sustain by delaying
Yusuf's intervention.

NOTES
================================================================
 
- Marshals (Alfonso, Yusuf): 4 Command cards instead of 3; can
  lead a Marching Group of multiple Lords; cannot be a Lower
  Lord under a Lieutenant.
 
- Call-to-Arms Lords (no Fealty rating): Yusuf, Sir, Eudes,
  both Rodrigos. They Muster only via the special Call to Arms
  procedure, never via a Fealty roll. See CALL TO ARMS
  REFERENCE above for the full procedure.
 
- The six Taifa Lords are: al-Mustain, Abu Bakr, Abd Allah,
  al-Mutawakkil, al-Mutamid, al-Mundir. Cards/effects targeting
  "Taifa" pennants apply to these six. Yusuf, Sir, and the two
  Rodrigos are NOT Taifa Lords and not affected by Taifa pennant
  cards/effects.
 
- "Abd Allah" / "Abdullah" are the same Arabic name (Abd Allah,
  "servant of God"); the game uses different romanizations
  per the convention each historical figure typically appears
  under in scholarship. The Lord [3] Abd Allah of Granada is a
  different person from al-Mustain's Vassal "Abd Allah, Emir
  of Alpuente" — they share a name only.
 
- Reconquista target rule (1.4.1): A Taifa flips to Reconquista
  status when ALL of its Cities AND all of its Taifa Lord
  printed Seats are Christian-Conquered. Yusuf's and Sir's
  Algeciras Seats do NOT count toward Sevilla Taifa Reconquista
  even though they are printed pennants — they are not Taifa
  Lords. Authoritative indicator: red cross-on-disc symbols
  printed on the map.
 
- Sevilla is special: 3x VP value at every Taifa status (3 VP
  Parias / 9 VP Reconquista vs. 1 VP / 3 VP for other Taifas).
  This is reflected on the map by Sevilla Taifa having 3
  status-tracking boxes instead of 1.
 
- Toledo Taifa has no Taifa Lord — historically, al-Qadir was
  too weak and subservient to Alfonso to be modeled. Toledo
  Taifa can therefore never be Independent; it is always either
  Parias or Reconquista.
