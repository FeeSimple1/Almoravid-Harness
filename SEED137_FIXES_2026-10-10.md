# Seed-137 capability repairs — October 10, 2026

Baseline: `090f224444a281bae690641322448b83d427d68d` (its engine source
matches the previously fixed `387c044`; the later merge added maintenance
files). The five defect groups were reproduced from the independent
Scenario F seed-137 evidence, not inferred only from code inspection.

## Repairs and authority

1. **M15 Saqalibah / M20 Al-Rûm scope.** Cards and eligibility now say
   This Lord, Taifa Muslim only. Both random Arts of War deployment and
   chosen Levy put the card on its holder's mat and enforce the existing
   personal-card limit. Handlers and legal menus require that holder.
   The Sagrajas minigame uses the same M15 scope and usage bookkeeping.
   Authority: Arts of War Reference M15/M20; Rules of Play 3.4.4.

2. **Troop-card lifetime.** A common Capability discard function removes
   the recorded contingent (never below zero) and clears usage exactly once.
   It is used at ordinary Disband, permanent/combat removal, Winter,
   Campaign-entry discard, and Wastage. Unused cards remove no troops.
   Continuing Winter Siege Lords retain their cards and usage. A subsequent
   acquisition can recruit again. Disbanded This-Lord cards return to the
   unused deck pool, never the active board-edge list.
   Authority: Arts of War M15/M20; Rules 3.3.1–3.3.2, 4.9.4, 6.3.1.

3. **Milites permanent removal.** Campaign-entry discard now invokes
   the same printed C18 rule as Winter: remove the whole card from the
   game while leaving its recruited troops. It cannot re-enter the draw
   or unused Capability pool. Discarding the Event half is unchanged.
   Authority: Arts of War C18 Milites.

4. **Fueros.** Only Alfonso may Levy C20; it remains a board-edge card.
   Inspection of the same card also revealed a Campaign-only timing gate.
   Its action/menu now operate once during each Levy, free, with an explicit
   one-or-two-marker choice. Strict-distance targeting is retained.
   Authority: Arts of War C20 Fueros and Tips; Rules 3.4.4.

5. **Player-selected Campaign excess.** A persisted Capability Discard
   step/pending decision asks Christians, then Muslims, which cards to lose.
   Each card is discoverable in the legal menu, or a batch can be submitted.
   No automatic choice is made. Planning/commands cannot bypass the decision;
   invalid, duplicate, wrong-side, and overlarge selections are atomic.
   Personal cards do not count. Authority: Rules of Play 4.0.

The action catalog and generated state schema are updated. No old-version
migration is added; an already corrupted save should be restarted or restored
from before the erroneous actions.
